Manga Genres Read

Open a Mihon backup and see what your library is actually made of. A title carries several genre tags at once, so every share here is a slice of all the tag assignments rather than of the titles.

It stays on your device

The backup is read in your browser and never uploaded. This page makes no network request except for its own fonts.

Where to find a backup

In Mihon: Settings, then Data and storage, then Create backup. The file it writes is named app.mihon_<date>.tachibk.

What you get

Genre share as a donut, the full ranking of every genre in the library, and a sortable table, all filterable by the source each title came from.

    The two percentages answer different questions. Share of tags divides by every tag assignment in view, so the slices add up to 100% and the donut is a true part-to-whole. On titles divides by the titles in view, and since one title carries several genres those figures add up to far more than 100%.
