Drop a Mihon backup on the page and find out what your library is really made
of:

- which genres actually dominate, as a share of every tag in the library
- chapters read, hours spent reading, and how many series you have finished
- publication status, plus your longest and most-read titles

Takes a `.tachibk`, a `.proto.gz` or a JSON-decoded backup. Everything is read
in your browser, nothing is uploaded, and anything a backup does not contain is
labelled as missing rather than counted as zero.
